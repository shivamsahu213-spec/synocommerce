# Merchandising Ranking Pipeline & Boost Rules

## Overview

Relevance scoring algorithm blending text match scores, Levenshtein typo distance, synonym expansion, brand boost multipliers, and pinned product positions.

---

## Ranking Pipeline Stages

1. Query Tokenization & Synonym Expansion.
2. Levenshtein Typo Matching (Distance <= 2).
3. Merchandising Boost Rules & Pinned Items Placement.
