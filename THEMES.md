# Themes

## Goal

Every client storefront should be composed by selecting a brand, a theme, and configuration values.

## Theme Scope

- Color tokens
- Typography tokens
- Shape and spacing tokens
- Component visual variants
- Layout presets

## Brand Scope

- Logos
- SEO defaults
- Social links
- Analytics identifiers
- Legal content references

## Rule

Theme and brand layers can influence presentation and metadata, but they must not contain core commerce business logic.
