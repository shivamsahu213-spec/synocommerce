# Vision

SynoCommerce will become the long-term commerce operating foundation for SynoStack Technologies.

## Product Direction

- One platform, many storefronts.
- Configuration-first client delivery.
- Module-based capability rollout.
- Headless-ready architecture for web, mobile, marketplaces, kiosks, and future AI channels.

## Non-Negotiables

- Enterprise maintainability over short-term speed.
- Predictable extensibility over one-off customization.
- Performance, accessibility, and security as defaults.
- Strong contracts between UI, domain logic, and backend APIs.
